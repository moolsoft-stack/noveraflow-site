Features

Records, symbols, flows, and AI feedback

NoveraFlow features are designed to help you notice flows over time instead of being interpreted too quickly.

Records

Write, edit, and revisit diary entries with text, photos, voice notes, and selected symbols.

Symbols

Symbols are not rewards or grades. They are small forms that grow beside records.

Flows

Record feelings with symbols and states, then revisit recurring themes through AI feedback and weekly or monthly reflections. This is not a clinical mood assessment.

AI feedback

Request AI feedback on a record. For a weekly or monthly reflection, use its card on Home when enough eligible records are available. Open a saved result to read it again. AI generation requires a connection and is subject to service limits.

Backup and restore

The app can export records as a backup ZIP and restore a supported backup. Exported files remain under the user's control.

Notifications

Notifications are designed to invite a quiet return, not pressure constant engagement.

NoveraFlow

A quiet diary that reflects flows instead of judging records.
