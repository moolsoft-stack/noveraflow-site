FAQ

How NoveraFlow works: free use, platforms and backup

Choose a journaling app with a clear view of what is free, where your entries are kept, and which features depend on the platform or installed version.

Start with free use and Premium, mobile and web differences, or backup and restore. For a short writing practice, see the self-reflection guide.

Start free on web for text entries and basic sync, or compare platform features before choosing the mobile app.

What is NoveraFlow?

NoveraFlow is a journaling app for everyday thoughts and self-reflection. Use the Android or iOS app for mobile diary features, or sign in on the web for text entries and basic sync. Features differ by platform and version.

Is AI feedback counseling or diagnosis?

No. AI feedback does not judge or diagnose records. It is provided as a reflective observation.

Where are records stored?

Storage depends on the platform and features you enable. The mobile app keeps local records; supported account-connected features also use servers. On the web, sign-in connects text entries to account-linked server storage and a browser device copy. Requested AI processing also sends relevant content to the server. Read the privacy policy before enabling optional features.

How do backup and restore work?

In the app’s data management settings, export a local backup ZIP or restore a supported backup. Keep the file yourself. Automatic cloud backup and cloud restore require a supported version, sign-in, and Premium access. Public availability varies by platform and version; cloud backup is not real-time sync.

Can notifications be turned off?

Yes. Notifications are controlled by the app setting and the device's notification permission.

Where can I contact support?

Product, privacy, and terms questions can be sent to the email on the contact page.

Can I talk with AI?

Maeumgyeol AI conversations require sign-in and Premium in supported mobile versions. Check your installed app before subscribing. AI conversations are not available on the web.

How do I write and manage diary entries?

On the web, sign in with Google or Apple, choose New entry, write a text entry and save. In the mobile app, create an entry with text, photos or a voice note, and choose a symbol or state if you want. Revisit saved entries in Records and open an entry to edit it.

How do weekly and monthly reflections work?

On Home, open the weekly or monthly reflection card after the completed period has enough eligible records. Request the reflection, or open an existing result. These are requested AI summaries, not automatic scheduled reports; a connection and service availability are required.

What is free, and what requires Premium?

Basic diary writing and supported local ZIP backup and restore are free in the mobile app. On the web, text writing, viewing, editing, search and basic sync are free after sign-in. AI feedback and weekly or monthly reflections have availability, access and usage limits; they are not unlimited. In supported mobile versions, AI conversations and automatic cloud backup require sign-in and Premium. Check the features and price shown in your installed app. Web does not offer AI, photo or voice entry, or backup and restore.

Which languages and platforms are supported?

The mobile app has Korean, English, and Japanese interfaces. Download it from Google Play or the App Store. The web workspace supports text entries and basic sync after sign-in; mobile AI, photos, voice and backup features are not available on the web.

Is an annual AI summary available?

We cannot yet confirm an available annual summary with a working generation and viewing flow. It is not included in the features we currently advertise. Weekly and monthly reflections are the supported period summaries.
