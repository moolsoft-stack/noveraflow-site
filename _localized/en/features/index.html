Features

A journaling practice for self-reflection

Start with one short journal entry. Keep what happened, notice what mattered, and return to your own words when you want to reflect on your day.

Keep an everyday journal

Write, edit, and revisit diary entries. The mobile app supports text, photos, voice notes and selected symbols. The web workspace supports text entries and basic sync after sign-in.

Symbols

Symbols are not rewards or grades. They are small forms that grow beside records.

Return to your thoughts

Read an earlier entry and notice what has changed or stayed with you. Symbols and states can sit beside your words; they are not scores or a clinical mood assessment.

Optional AI reflections

In supported mobile versions, request feedback on an entry or a weekly or monthly reflection when enough eligible records are available. AI needs a connection and is subject to access and usage limits. It does not diagnose you. AI features are not offered in the web workspace.

Backup and restore

In the mobile app, export and restore a supported local backup ZIP for free. Keep exported files safely. Automatic cloud backup is a separate Premium feature in supported versions and requires sign-in; it is not real-time sync. Backup and restore are not available on the web.

Notifications

Notifications are designed to invite a quiet return, not pressure constant engagement.

A small reflection you can return to

  1. Write one moment in your own words. A sentence is enough.
  2. Add what you noticed or felt, if you want to.
  3. Return to the entry later and see what still matters.

You can write your own questions in an ordinary text entry: What happened? What mattered to me? What would I like to remember?
