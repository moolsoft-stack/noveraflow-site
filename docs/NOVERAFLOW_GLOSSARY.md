## Tone Rules

- Korean: use natural polite language. Avoid exaggerated promotional claims.
- English: keep sentences short and clear. Use product explanation language,
  not growth-hacking copy.
- Japanese: use natural app/service guidance language. Avoid overly literal
  Korean sentence structure.
- AI feedback must not sound like diagnosis, counseling, or prescription.
- Symbols must not be described as rewards, grades, fortune, or personality
  types.
