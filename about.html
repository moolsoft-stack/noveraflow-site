About NoveraFlow

NoveraFlow is a quiet AI diary for people who want small records to become personal flows without turning life into a score.

Records come first

A record does not need to be long or complete. A short sentence, a symbol, a photo, or a voice note can be enough to keep a trace of the day.

App guide and download

NoveraFlow is available for Android on Google Play and for iPhone and iPad on the App Store. The iOS 1.0.80 (81) update is under Apple review; review-build features are not yet confirmed in the public iOS app.

Backup and restore

Export a local backup ZIP and restore a supported backup in the app. Keep exported files safely. Automatic cloud backup is a separate, version-dependent Premium feature; it is not real-time device sync.
